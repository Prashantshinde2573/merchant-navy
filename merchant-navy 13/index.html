Scope of Work

Multiple port services required for MV Indravati (45,000 DWT bulk carrier) arriving Visakhapatnam. Vendors with VPT authorisation and valid certifications only. Quotations invited for one or more services.

Vessel: MV Indravati | Bulk Carrier | 45,000 DWT | Crew: 22

Port: Visakhapatnam (INVTZ) | ETA: ~5 days | Berth window: 48 hours

Services required:

Ship Chandlering — Provisions for 22 crew (30-day stock, vegetarian mandatory) + deck and engine stores. Delivery alongside within 12 hours of berthing.

Fresh Water Supply — ~150 MT potable water. WHO-standard quality certificate mandatory. Metered barge or pipeline delivery.

Bunker Supply — VLSFO ~400 MT + MGO ~40 MT. Barge alongside. MARPOL-compliant BDN and sealed samples required.

Crew Transfer — 8 off-signers + 8 on-signers. Launch at anchorage or road transfer at berth (quote both). Immigration coordination expected.

Waste Disposal — MARPOL Annex I sludge ~8 MT, Annex V solid waste ~1.5 MT, food waste ~200 kg. Receipts and disposal certificates mandatory.

Vendor requirements:

VPT-licensed / port authority authorised

Valid insurance and applicable certifications

Itemised quote per service

Ability to coordinate within the 48-hour berth window

Payment: Net 15 days from invoice | Quote deadline: 48 hours | GST applicable
